Privacy policy

Your journal is yours.

The short version: what you write stays on your phone.

What Journal keeps, and where

Your diary, notes, Flow thoughts, tasks and settings are stored in a database on your device. There is no Journal account and no Journal server, so we never receive, read or store your entries.

The AI

Summaries, questions, reflections and other AI features run either on your phone or on a computer you pair yourself with the Journal Companion. Pairing happens over your own local network, is encrypted, and needs your approval on both devices. Your words are not sent to an AI service on the internet.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, Journal backs up your notes, diaries and thoughts to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Journal can see that folder. Photos, voice recordings and other attachments are not uploaded. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account and Drive. You can sign out at any time, and the app keeps your journal on the device.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos, only when you add a photo or scan a page.
  • Microphone, only while you are talking to your diary.
  • Local network, only to reach a computer you have paired.
  • Internet, only for the optional Google Drive backup and for downloading AI models you choose. Downloading a model sends nothing from your journal.

No tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

This website

This site has no accounts, cookies or analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. If you write your name on the book's cover, it is saved only in your own browser.

Children

Journal is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
